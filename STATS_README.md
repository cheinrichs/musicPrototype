🌱 1. Sound Awareness
Learning Goal:	Develop basic auditory discrimination and awareness of sound properties (volume, duration, presence, attack, release).
	Children learn that sound has qualities — it can be loud, soft, long, short, sudden, smooth, or silent. This builds the foundation for all later musical perception.

🎵 2. Pitch Awareness		
	Learning Goal:	Recognize and compare differences in pitch without needing symbolic labels.
		Children learn to hear “higher vs lower,” match notes, and sense pitch distance. This builds relative pitch intuition before note names or theory.

🌈 3. Contour & Shape		
	Learning Goal:	Understand how melodies move through space over time.
		Children learn to perceive melodic direction and shape (up, down, arch, zigzag), connecting sound movement to visual and embodied metaphors.


⏳ 4. Rhythm & Timing		
	Learning Goal:	Develop awareness of pulse, timing, and rhythmic patterns.
		Children learn to feel steady beat, anticipate accents, and imitate short rhythmic sequences. This builds temporal coordination and musical timing.


🎭 5. Anticipation & Musical Punctuation		
	Learning Goal:	Recognize pauses, accents, and musical endings.
		Children learn that silence, buildup, and resolution are meaningful parts of music. This builds anticipation, listening patience, and phrase awareness.

🎶 6. Tonal Contrast & Resolution		
	Learning Goal:	Perceive emotional color and tonal stability in music.
		Children learn to hear differences in mood (happy/sad), tension and release, and the feeling of “home” in a melody or chord progression.

🎤 7. Sound Making & Sound Shaping		
	Learning Goal:	Gain control over producing and shaping sound intentionally.
		Children learn to control attack, sustain, release, volume, airflow, and vocal expression. This builds embodied musicianship and expressive confidence.


🎻 8. Timbre & Instrument Awareness		
	Learning Goal:	Recognize and explore differences in sound color across voices and instruments.
		Children learn that the same pitch can sound different depending on the instrument. This broadens listening skills beyond piano-centric hearing.

🎹 9. Guided Instrument Play		
	Learning Goal:	Translate listening and visual cues into coordinated musical execution.
		Children learn to follow patterns, sequence notes, and gradually perform simple musical phrases with expression.

🎼 10. Harmony & Chord Feel		
	Learning Goal:	Perceive how multiple notes combine vertically to create emotional color and stability.
		Children learn to hear chord quality (major/minor), chord movement, and the feeling of harmonic resolution.

🎵 11. Pitch Naming & Functional Hearing		
	Learning Goal:	Connect pitch perception to symbolic language (e.g., solfege).
		Children learn to associate sounds with names and understand tonal relationships within a scale.
